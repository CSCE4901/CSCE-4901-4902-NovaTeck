Privacy Policy

NovaTeck helps students explore Dallas–Fort Worth technology jobs and compare their skills with employer requirements. This policy explains how the NovaTeck project team handles information you provide while using the app.

Accounts and job activity

NovaTeck uses your name and email to manage your account and sends email for account verification and password reset. Passwords are stored as hashes. Profile information you enter, saved jobs, job activity, and reminders help the app show your comparisons and track opportunities. Support requests are saved for the project team to review.

Resumes

NovaTeck saves the resume file you upload and extracts skills for review and matching. Choose a sample resume if you are testing the app. Leave out sensitive information that the app does not need.

Nova chat

When you send a message to Nova, the app sends the conversation to Google Gemini. Profile skills are included when you choose to attach them. Your saved resume and job history are not attached automatically. For unpaid Gemini API usage, Google may use submitted content and responses to improve its services under its terms. Keep personal and confidential information out of chat. Google Gemini API terms

Hosting and browser storage

AWS hosts the public app and Aiven hosts its database. NovaTeck uses browser storage to maintain your sign-in session and remember display preferences. Employer websites have their own privacy policies.

Your information and requests

You can edit your profile or replace your resume in the app. For questions about retention, access, corrections, or deletion, contact the project team through Contact Support or email novateck.support@gmail.com. Include the email address associated with your account and describe your request. Keep passwords and resume files out of the message.
