Terms of Use

NovaTeck is a capstone project that helps students find and compare Dallas–Fort Worth technology jobs. These terms apply when you use the app.

Accounts and uploads

Use an email address you control and keep your password private. Upload resumes and other material you have permission to share. Use the app for job discovery and career planning. Avoid impersonation, harassment, and attempts to access another person’s account or information.

Job listings and applications

Employers can change or remove listings at any time. Confirm the details on the employer’s website before applying. When you select Apply, the employer’s website handles the application and its terms apply there.

Skill matches and AI guidance

Match scores compare available skill information with extracted job requirements. They can miss qualifications or context. Nova’s AI replies can contain errors. Check important information with the employer or original source before relying on it.
